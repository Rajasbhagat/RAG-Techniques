# TechClean Solutions: Growth Strategy Dilemma

**Date:** January 15, 2025  
**Subject:** Strategic Management  
**Protagonist:** Sarah Chen, CEO

## Background
TechClean Solutions, founded in 2018 in Austin, Texas, specializes in eco-friendly cleaning robots for industrial warehouses. By 2024, the company had captured 15% of the US warehouse market, generating $45M in annual revenue with a 20% EBITDA margin.

## The Problem
Growth has slowed to 5% YoY as the US market saturates. Sarah Chen is facing pressure from investors to reach $100M revenue by 2027.

## Strategic Options

### Option 1: International Expansion (Europe)
- **Pros**: Large untapped market, high demand for green tech.
- **Cons**: High regulatory hurdles (EU standards), significant upfront logistics cost ($10M).
- **Projected Revenue**: $30M by 2027.

### Option 2: New Product Line (Home Consumer Market)
- **Pros**: Leverage existing technology, massive market size.
- **Cons**: Highly competitive (iRobot, Dyson), dilute brand identity (B2B vs B2C), lower margins (10%).
- **Projected Revenue**: $50M by 2027 (but risky).

### Option 3: Service Model Pivot (RaaS - Robots as a Service)
- **Pros**: Recurring revenue, sticky customers, lower barrier to entry for smaller warehouse clients.
- **Cons**: Cash flow crunch in years 1-2 due to hardware financing.
- **Projected Revenue**: $20M incremental, but higher valuation multiple.

## Decision Point
The board meets in one week. Sarah needs to present a recommendation that balances growth, risk, and profitability.
